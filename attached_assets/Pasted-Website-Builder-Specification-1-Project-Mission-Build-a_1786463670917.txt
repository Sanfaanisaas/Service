Website Builder Specification
1. Project Mission

Build a production-oriented full-stack web application called SANFAANI Operations.

SANFAANI is a small-business operations and bookkeeping platform combining:

Inventory / Stock Management
Device Charging Management
Workspace Management
Central Bookkeeping / Revenue Ledger
Customer Check-in
Receipts and secure device claim IDs
Notifications and reminders
Daily operational history
Administrative dashboard
Customer-facing portal

The application should replace manual notebooks and fragmented records with one reliable digital operating system.

Do not build this as a static dashboard or UI prototype.

Every important action must connect to real application state, backend APIs, authentication, MongoDB persistence, validation, and authorization.

OUT OF STOCK

47. Prevent Race Conditions

Charging capacity enforcement must not rely solely on:

activeSessions.length < 40

followed later by an insert without protection.

Two staff devices could check someone in simultaneously.

Implement server-side concurrency protection.

Likewise prevent:

duplicate slot allocation
negative inventory
duplicate claim IDs

Treat these as system invariants.
